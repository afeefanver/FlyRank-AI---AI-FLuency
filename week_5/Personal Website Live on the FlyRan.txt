Why it matters: A personal website is the one profile no platform can take away, and shipping it teaches you hosting, HTTPS, and basic web infrastructure every track benefits from. You build it now on a free host, with a public URL you can send to anyone today.

At the end of the track, once your capstone is approved, you will receive an official FlyRank completion badge to add to your website. The site remains yours, on the hosting setup and domain you choose.

Brief
Plan a simple site: who you are, what you are building, links to your LinkedIn, GitHub, CV, and booking link, and a space for future posts and capstone work. One page is enough to start.

Build it on a free hosting path. Netlify is the recommended default: Netlify supports this internship program, the free tier covers everything you will do here, HTTPS is automatic, and you can connect your own custom domain later if you choose. GitHub Pages (pairs with PF-05), Cloudflare Pages, and Vercel's free hobby tier are all accepted too. Hand-written HTML, a static site generator, or an AI-assisted build are all fine; you must understand every file you deploy.

Get it live over HTTPS on your host's free URL, then rename the site to something you would put on a CV: yourname.netlify.app, not spontaneous-kitten-3f21.netlify.app. On Netlify that is Site configuration → Change site name. This URL is the deliverable for this assignment.

Write your DNS walkthrough: explain what DNS does, what a CNAME record is, and what actually happens between someone typing a website address and the host answering — resolver, nameserver, record, response. Explain it so a non-technical member of the team could follow it.

You do not need to purchase or configure a custom domain for this assignment. The goal is to understand the infrastructure well enough that, if you connect your own domain in the future, you know what is happening rather than simply following instructions.

Link the site from your LinkedIn profile and CV. Use your live hosting URL. If you later connect your own custom domain, you can update those links.

Your FlyRank completion badge
Once you successfully complete the track and your capstone is approved, you will receive an official FlyRank completion badge.

Add the badge to your personal website as part of your final capstone update. It serves as recognition that you completed the FlyRank internship program and gives visitors to your site a visible connection between your portfolio work and the program.

Instructions and the badge asset will be provided when your capstone is approved. There is nothing you need to request or configure now.
